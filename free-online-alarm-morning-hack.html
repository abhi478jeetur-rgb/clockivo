You don't need a fancy gadget to fix this. You just need physical distance. This is exactly where an alarm clock free online becomes your best friend.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will a free online alarm clock work if my computer goes to sleep?
For the best results, you should adjust your computer's power settings to ensure it doesn't go to "Sleep" or "Hibernate" mode while the browser is running. You can easily set your display to turn off, but keep the computer awake so the alarm sounds exactly on time.

Does leaving a browser alarm on drain a lot of electricity?
Not at all! Running a simple browser tab uses very minimal processing power. If you turn your screen brightness down or let the display turn off naturally, the energy consumption is negligible.

Is an online alarm clock better than my phone?
Yes, primarily because of the psychological barrier it creates. Using a web-based clock separates your wake-up tool from your communication device, helping you avoid the temptation to check emails or social media the very second you open your eyes.
