// Help

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Clockivo?

Clockivo is a premium, browser-based time utility suite designed with a liquid-glass aesthetic. It combines Alarms, Timers, Stopwatch, and World Clock in one sleek interface.

Will the alarm ring if I close the tab?

No. Due to browser security limitations, the website must be open in a tab to trigger alarms. However, it can run perfectly in the background or on a minimized window.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. All your alarms, settings, and world clocks are stored locally on your device using browser Storage. No data is ever sent to any server.

How do I export my Stopwatch laps?

In the Stopwatch section, once you have recorded laps, a "Export CSV" button will appear in the Laps panel. Click it to download your data in a spreadsheet-friendly format.

Can I customize the theme?

Yes, you can switch between Light and Dark modes in the Settings section. The "Auto" mode follows your system's preferences automatically.

Your Data Stays Yours

At Clockivo, we value your privacy above all else. Our application is designed as a zero-backend architecture. This means:

  • We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data.
  • All your alarms, settings, and timers are saved locally on your device using your browser's localStorage.
  • No analytical tracking or intrusive cookies are deployed.
